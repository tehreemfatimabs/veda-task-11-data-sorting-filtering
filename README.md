# VEDA Technology Internship — Task 11
## Basic Data Sorting & Filtering in Excel

### Project Overview
This project was completed as part of the VEDA Technology Internship. It demonstrates how Excel sorting and filtering techniques can be used to explore sales data, answer business questions, and identify relevant records for business analysis.

### Objectives
- Practice sorting sales data in ascending and descending order.
- Apply number filters to identify high-value transactions.
- Use multiple filters to identify records matching specific business conditions.
- Explore regional sales performance.
- Document findings and business insights in a structured Excel worksheet.

### Tools Used
- Microsoft Excel
- Sorting and AutoFilter
- Number Filters
- Multiple-column filtering
- PivotTable for regional sales analysis

### Business Questions
1. Which orders have the highest sales?
2. Which orders have sales greater than $10,000?
3. Which region generates the most sales?
4. How many records match a specific category and region?
5. Which records contain a quantity greater than 10 units?

### Key Findings
- Identified the five highest-sales orders by sorting sales in descending order.
- Used a sales threshold of $10,000 to identify high-value transactions.
- Examined regional sales performance using a PivotTable.
- Identified 599 records matching the selected category and region filters, out of 9,994 records.
- Identified 113 records with quantities greater than 10 units, out of 9,994 records.

### Workflow
1. Preserved the original dataset.
2. Applied sorting and filtering to the working data.
3. Used multiple filters to narrow down relevant records.
4. Reviewed the resulting records and counts.
5. Documented the results and business insights in the `Business_Questions` worksheet.

### Key Learning Outcomes
- Understanding the difference between sorting and filtering.
- Applying multiple conditions to explore business data.
- Identifying high-value transactions and relevant records.
- Using PivotTables to compare regional sales.
- Communicating analytical results clearly.
